Format

[edit]
  • Swiss Stage
    • 16 team swiss format, Bo3
      • Three (3) won match series lead to advancement to playoffs.
      • Three (3) lost match series lead to elimination from the swiss stage.
  • Relegation
    • Bottom eight (8) teams from Swiss Stage
      • Winning teams (4) qualify for Season 3
      • Losing teams (4) are relegated to Division 2
  • Playoffs
    • Top eight (8) teams from Swiss Stage
    • Single Elimination, Bo5
