Method & verification

How every pick & win probability is originated

Fixtures come from Hotspawn. Probabilities never do. Every fixture passes through a verified 5-tier cascade: external bookmaker consensus → tier-anchored Elo → split standings → competition prior → explicit 50/50 placeholder.

1. Fixture discovery & lobby reconciliation (every 6 hours)

We reconcile Hotspawn's LoL Pick'em lobby with Hotspawn's backend fixtures feed (/api/v1/fixtures) every 6 hours via GitHub Actions cron (0 */6 * * *). Cancelled or withdrawn upstream rows are excluded from pricing and audited on Flagged for review (check D21). Exact UTC timestamps come directly from millisecond epoch fields.

2. External market consensus (deduplicated 1 main-match line per bookmaker)

For every fixture we query independent bookmaker and prediction-market feeds — Pinnacle, Bovada, Unibet (Kambi) and Polymarket. Child prop/special boards (kills, towers, barons, dragons, correct score, map-N child markets on series) are strictly rejected, and each bookmaker contributes at most one full-match moneyline per fixture so no single feed can echo-dominate the median (check D20).

Access note: Polymarket's homepage (checked 2026-10-23, polymarket.com) states that trading is blocked in the United States and offers a view-only mode. Its prices are used here solely as public reference data; this site does not place or recommend bets.

  • Decimal/American prices are two-way de-vigged so probabilities sum to 100.0%.
  • Consensus = exact median across ≥3 independent books, or fixed-quality weighted mean (Pinnacle 1.0, Unibet 0.85, Bovada 0.8, Polymarket 0.7) for 2 books.
  • The displayed dispersion is the gap between the highest and lowest source probability (≤6 pts = High confidence, ≤10 = Medium, >10 = Low).
  • One book counts once (check D20): repeated boards per book + team pair + start hour are de-duplicated before consensus is computed, prop boards (kills, maps, towers, barons, handicaps) are rejected, and single-game markets are blocked on Bo3/Bo5 series (check D16).

3. Own Elo model + Split Standings fallback + Entity disambiguation

Because no accessible sportsbook prices Tier-3/national divisions (PRM 3rd Division, RIVALS League, Tunisian Super League, Komplettligaen, Arabian 2nd Division, LigaGG), we originate probabilities from Leaguepedia's recorded match results and split standings:

Not an official results source: Leaguepedia is a community-maintained database, not Riot or an event organizer. Model probabilities are estimates derived from community records, not official odds or organizer-verified win probabilities, and Hotspawn confirms only the listed fixture.
  • Chronological Tier-Anchored Elo (MODEL_ELO / MODEL_ELO_THIN): Base K = 24 (×0.8 Bo1, ×1.0 Bo3, ×1.3 Bo5+, margin multiplier up to ×1.5) over 24 months of completed series on Leaguepedia (MatchSchedule), decayed toward competition-strength priors with a 365-day half-life, smoothed with head-to-head (≤15% weight with ≥3 meetings), and shrunk toward 50/50 on small samples (games/(games+8)).
  • Current-split Standings Fallback (MODEL_STANDINGS 📊): When Elo lacks games on a side yet Leaguepedia's Standings table records current-split series (WinSeries/LossSeries), a Laplace-smoothed series/game win-rate becomes an Elo offset from the competition prior (prior + 400·log10(wr/(1−wr)), wr clamped to 0.15–0.85) and is shrunk by sample size (0–0 records excluded; always Low confidence because it ignores strength of schedule).
  • Competition-Scoped Disambiguation & Schedule Bridging: Resolves parenthetical disambiguations within the verified competition roster (e.g. LYON (2024 American Team) at Worlds, Locked In (Dutch Team)) and bridges upstream Hotspawn tag collisions (e.g. Phong Vũ Buffalo → PvB (Swiss Team)), flagging HOTSPAWN_NAME_COLLISION / NAME_BRIDGED_VIA_SCHEDULE.
  • Competition Prior (MODEL_LEAGUE_PRIOR) & Unresolved (UNRESOLVED): One missing side uses the competition median rating with halved shrink; zero-evidence matches stay strictly 50.0% / 50.0% placeholder.

4. Out-of-sample validation

Ratings are fitted strictly prior to a 30-day holdout window, frozen, and scored out-of-sample on completed matches against coin-flip log-loss (0.6931) and Brier (0.2500) baselines.

5. Data attribution & licensing

  • Leaguepedia (lol.fandom.com, Fandom): Community match schedules, recorded series results, team pages and split standings licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.
  • Liquipedia (liquipedia.net MediaWiki API): Tournament index metadata queried strictly via the official MediaWiki API (api.php, gzip, ≥2.2s pacing, never HTML scraping) under CC-BY-SA 3.0.
  • Hotspawn (play.hotspawn.com): Used strictly for fixture discovery (teams, competition, UTC lock time). Hotspawn crowd percentages are never used in any probability calculation.

Update cadence & verification

The pipeline runs on a GitHub Actions schedule every six hours: it fetches the Hotspawn fixture list, external prices and Leaguepedia results, rebuilds the model, verifies that every fixture has either a market line or a model output, and publishes data/picks.json. Each run records its source status above; if a fetch fails the run is flagged rather than publishing partial or invented numbers. Every table row links the exact page used for its values.
