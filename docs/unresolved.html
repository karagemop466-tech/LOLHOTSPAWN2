Unresolved picks & limitations for generating picks

Every match is listed; a pick is shown only when there is a market or model signal. This page documents the ones that cannot be priced the way the featured picks are: no usable bookmaker or prediction-market line was found by our adapters, so the number comes from our own model on recorded results, or — when even that evidence is missing — it stays an explicit 50.0% / 50.0% placeholder, never an edge or team recommendation. Hotspawn crowd percentages are never used.

Model estimates use community-reported records. They do not satisfy independent primary-organizer verification of each result. See every affected fixture and its blank official-confirmation field. A blocked or empty endpoint cannot establish that no bookmaker offers a line.

What we do instead of guessing

  • External betting lines first: When Pinnacle, Unibet (Kambi), Bovada, or Polymarket list a full-match two-way market, we use the de-vigged consensus.
  • Chronological Tier-Anchored Elo: When no sportsbook prices a competition, our Elo model rates both teams from recorded Leaguepedia series anchored to competition tier.
  • Split standings (MODEL_STANDINGS 📊): when Elo cannot rate a side yet both teams have a current-split record in Leaguepedia's Standings table, smoothed Bradley–Terry series/game rates produce a bounded estimate capped at Low confidence.
  • Elo + standings blend (MODEL_ELO_STANDINGS 📊🧮): when a side has only a thin Elo history (<8 games in 365d) but a current-split record for this competition, that record is blended into the rating (capped at Medium confidence, flagged STANDINGS_BLEND_APPLIED). A record from a different competition is never used.
  • One side unknown (MODEL_LEAGUE_PRIOR): The competition's median rating stands in for the missing side, shrunk toward 50/50 and flagged ONE_TEAM_NO_HISTORY.
  • Both sides unknown (UNRESOLVED): Stays strictly 50.0% / 50.0% placeholder and never masquerades as a model edge.
