Transparency

Unresolved & low-confidence picks

Every match on Hotspawn's list gets a published pick — but a pick is only as good as its inputs. When there is no external line and no usable recorded history, the row is unresolved: we show an explicit 50/50 placeholder, label it, and explain it here. It is not an edge or a recommendation.

Why this page exists. Bookmakers and prediction markets price the top competitions — the World Championship, the EMEA Masters, the major regional leagues. They do not price most national leagues, third divisions or amateur cups. Leaguepedia records results unevenly at that level, and brand-new rosters have no games at all. Rather than invent a number, we publish the placeholder and tell you which input was missing. Hotspawn's crowd percentage is never used as a substitute.

What we do instead of guessing

  • No line, no problem — as long as there is history. Our Elo model uses recorded results and is anchored to the strength tier of the competition each team plays in.
  • One side unknown? We use the competition's median rating (or its tier prior) for the missing side and mark the row MODEL_LEAGUE_PRIOR / ONE_TEAM_NO_HISTORY.
  • Both sides unknown? The row stays unresolved (50/50 placeholder) and appears above — it is never dressed up as a model output.
  • Verification. Every row links its Hotspawn fixture and either the bookmaker/market page or the Leaguepedia tournament page used. No value is inferred without a link.
