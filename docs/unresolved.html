What we do instead of guessing

  • External betting lines first: when Pinnacle, Unibet (Kambi), Bovada or Polymarket list a full-match two-way market, we use the de-vigged consensus.
  • Tier-anchored Elo: when no bookmaker prices a competition, our Elo model rates both teams from recorded Leaguepedia series anchored to the competition's strength tier.
  • Split standings (MODEL_STANDINGS 📊): when Elo cannot rate a side yet both teams have a current-split record, the smoothed series/game rates give a bounded estimate, capped at Low/Medium confidence.
  • One side unknown (MODEL_LEAGUE_PRIOR): the competition's median rating stands in for the missing side, shrunk toward 50/50 and flagged ONE_TEAM_NO_HISTORY.
  • Both sides unknown (UNRESOLVED): stays a strict 50.0% / 50.0% placeholder and never masquerades as a model edge.
