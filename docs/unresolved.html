What we do instead of guessing

  • External betting lines first: When Pinnacle, Unibet (Kambi), Bovada, or Polymarket list a full-match two-way market, we use the de-vigged consensus.
  • Chronological Tier-Anchored Elo: When no sportsbook prices a competition, our Elo model rates both teams from recorded Leaguepedia series anchored to competition tier.
  • Official standings record (MODEL_STANDINGS 📊): When Elo cannot rate a side yet both teams have current-split records in Leaguepedia's Standings table, smoothed Bradley–Terry series/game rates produce a bounded estimate capped at Low/Medium confidence.
  • One side unknown (MODEL_LEAGUE_PRIOR): The competition's median rating stands in for the missing side, shrunk toward 50/50 and flagged ONE_TEAM_NO_HISTORY.
  • Both sides unknown (UNRESOLVED): Stays strictly 50.0% / 50.0% placeholder and never masquerades as a model edge.
