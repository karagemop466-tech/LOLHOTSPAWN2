Transparency & limitations

Unresolved & low-confidence picks

Every match on Hotspawn's list gets a published pick — never invented values. When an amateur or debut match lacks both external betting lines and recorded history/standings, it stays an explicit 50.0% / 50.0% unresolved placeholder with its exact reason and source link. Hotspawn's crowd percentage is never used as a substitute.

How non-market matches are resolved first: Before marking any fixture unresolved, the pipeline checks (1) de-vigged lines from Pinnacle, Bovada, Unibet (Kambi) and Polymarket, (2) chronological Elo from Leaguepedia's recorded series results with competition-scoped entity disambiguation and exact UTC schedule bridging, (3) smoothed Bradley-Terry series/game win rates from Leaguepedia's official split Standings tables, and (4) tournament median prior when exactly one side has verified history (MODEL_LEAGUE_PRIOR).
