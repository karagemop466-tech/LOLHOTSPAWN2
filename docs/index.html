Executive summary

Every LoL match on Hotspawn's Pick'em list — with a pick and a win probability.

This site mirrors the League of Legends fixtures on Hotspawn's Pick'em lobby and prices every one of them: matches covered by established bookmakers use the external market consensus; low-tier and amateur matches, which no book prices, are priced by our own model from recorded match results and the official league win-loss table. Hotspawn's crowd percentages are shown as context only and are never an input to a probability.
