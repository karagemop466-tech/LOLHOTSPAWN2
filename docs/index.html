Executive summary

Every LoL match on Hotspawn's Pick'em list — with a pick and a win probability.

Fixtures mirror Hotspawn's Pick'em lobby (refreshed every 6 hours). Every match is priced: 💰 external bookmaker/market lines where they exist, otherwise 🧮 our Elo model or 📊 official standings records. Hotspawn crowd % is context only, never a probability.

Source limitation: When there is no external market line, the probability is our model estimate from Leaguepedia's recorded results. Leaguepedia is a community-maintained results database, not an official tournament or Riot source; Hotspawn confirms the listed fixture, not its win probability. These estimates are not official lines. See source limitations and flagged items.

Unresolved picks & limitations

Anything we cannot verify is published as an explicit placeholder and listed on its own page — never silently filled in. Matches with no bookmaker line are priced from recorded results first (Elo), then from the official standings record in that league (📊), then from a league prior; the per-league table on the Unresolved page shows exactly which signal prices each league and where its limits are.

Featured — matches with external betting lines

These rows use the de-vigged consensus of the bookmakers and prediction markets listed on each card; the model is shown alongside as a cross-check.
