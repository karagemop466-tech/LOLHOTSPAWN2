How every pick is priced — featured vs non-featured matches

  • 💰 Featured (external betting lines first): if Pinnacle, Unibet (Kambi), Bovada or Polymarket list a full-series two-way market, the pick is their de-vigged consensus — one deduplicated line per book. These cover Worlds and top tier-2 events only.
  • 🧮 Our own Elo line: low-tier / amateur matches with no bookmaker or Polymarket coverage are priced by a tier-anchored Elo model built from 24 months of recorded Leaguepedia series. Thin history (<8 games) is labelled low confidence, never hidden.
  • 📊 Split standings: if a team has no Elo rating yet but has a current-split record in Leaguepedia Standings, that record becomes a smoothed rating offset, capped at Low confidence.
  • 📐 League prior: if one side has no history at all, its competition's median rating stands in, shrunk toward 50/50 and flagged.
  • ⚠️ Unresolved: if neither side has any verified signal, the row stays an explicit 50.0% / 50.0% placeholder — see the unresolved section below and the full limitations page. Full formulas: Methodology & sources.
