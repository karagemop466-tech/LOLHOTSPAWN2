Source limitations: official vs. community data

External market probabilities link directly to the named bookmaker or prediction market. For fixtures without a market line, our Elo / standings estimates use recorded results from Leaguepedia, a community-maintained database rather than an official Riot or tournament-organizer odds feed. The Hotspawn fixture link confirms the fixture listing only, never win probability.

All model-only picks are marked NO_EXTERNAL_MARKET. Unverified tiers, missing Hotspawn match pages, upstream tag collisions, thin history, and schedule differences carry explicit row-level flags below.

What each flag means

HOTSPAWN_NAME_COLLISION
Hotspawn expands an abbreviated team tag to a different/defunct team (e.g. Phong Vũ Buffalo for DACH entrant PvB (Swiss Team) in PRM 2nd Division); priced using the verified split entrant.
HOTSPAWN_ENTITY_MISMATCH
Hotspawn's upstream feed mapped a team tag to an unrelated defunct/foreign team; verified via unanimous exact UTC schedule slots on Leaguepedia.
NAME_BRIDGED_VIA_SCHEDULE
Rebranded/acronym team name matched to its Leaguepedia split entrant via 2+ exact UTC schedule slots in the same competition.
LEAGUEPEDIA_TIME_TBD
Leaguepedia records the split matchup without a confirmed timestamp yet; Hotspawn's epoch timestamp governs pick lock.
NO_EXTERNAL_MARKET
No bookmaker or prediction-market line was found for this fixture, so the pick comes from our Elo/standings model (or is unresolved).
NO_TEAM_HISTORY
Neither team has recorded games or split standings; published as an explicit 50/50 placeholder.
ONE_TEAM_NO_HISTORY
One side has no usable history, so the model substitutes the competition median or tier prior and shrinks toward 50/50.
THIN_HISTORY
Fewer than five recorded games in 365 days for at least one team — confidence is capped.
CROSS_LEAGUE_MODEL
The two teams' recent competitions differ across ecosystem tiers; confidence is capped at Low.
STANDINGS_BLEND_APPLIED
The side had only a thin Elo history, so its current-split record in this same competition was blended into the rating (method MODEL_ELO_STANDINGS). The split record is weighted by how many decided series it contains, capped at 60% of the rating, and the row is capped at Medium confidence.
MODEL_DISAGREES_WITH_MARKET
The model differs from the external bookmaker consensus by ≥8 percentage points in the same competition. Published pick follows the market; gap is shown.
MARKET_DISAGREEMENT
Independent bookmaker/market sources disagree by more than 10 probability points.
START_TIME_DISAGREES_WITH_BOOKS
A bookmaker's listed start time differs from Hotspawn's epoch timestamp by >90 minutes.
START_TIME_MISMATCH
Hotspawn and Leaguepedia differ on start time by >30 minutes (common on flex-scheduled amateur matches; Hotspawn governs pick lock).
NO_HOTSPAWN_MATCH_PAGE
Hotspawn's API lists the fixture without a standalone permalink; links point cleanly to the official lobby instead of a 404.
TIER_UNVERIFIED
Hotspawn still marks the competition tier as "waiting for information".
GENERIC_RESULTS_SOURCE
No fixture-specific Leaguepedia competition page was matched for this model row; its recorded-results source link is a general Leaguepedia table.
NO_VERIFIED_SIGNAL
No external market, no Elo history, and no standings — explicit 50/50 placeholder.
